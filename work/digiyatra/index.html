Paperless, biometric airport check-in for India

DigiYatra lets travellers move through Indian airports using face recognition instead of paper boarding passes. I designed complex user scenarios and interfaces for Phase 2, with accessibility built in from the start.

Context

A national system serves every kind of traveller: first-time flyers, frequent business travellers, older people and people with disabilities. Each needs to get through the airport without help.

Complex scenarios

I mapped and designed for the complex user scenarios that a biometric system has to handle, then turned them into clear interfaces.

03

At national scale, accessibility is not optional. It was a core requirement from the first sketches, not a check at the end.
