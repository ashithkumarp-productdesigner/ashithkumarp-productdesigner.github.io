01

Context

ZAPS combines luxury mobility with a loyalty programme. The app needed to make booking quick and keep members coming back to earn and redeem points.

02

Structure first

I defined the experience strategy and information architecture, then designed the core user journeys on top of it.

03

Designing with data

I rebuilt the engagement workflows around behavioural analytics and experimentation, testing changes against real usage rather than opinion.

This cut the average time to book by 2 minutes and lifted point redemption by 48%.

04

Two languages, one experience

The app has full English and Arabic support. Arabic reads right to left, so layouts, navigation and icons mirror rather than just swapping the text.

05

Impact

  • +48% loyalty point redemption.
  • 2 minutes faster to book, on average.
  • A bilingual app reaching real users at scale.
